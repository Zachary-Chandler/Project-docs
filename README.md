# Project Mind Maps

This repository contains my personal **project mind maps**, which I use to document and keep track of my work throughout different projects.

These mind maps are meant to capture more than just the final results. They document the **successes, failures, challenges, completed work, experiments, and lessons learned** throughout a project.

## Purpose

The goal of this repository is to create a record of what I worked on and how projects developed over time.

Each mind map can be used to track:

* What was completed by me
* What I was responsible for
* Successful implementations
* Failed attempts and what went wrong
* Problems and challenges encountered
* Testing and experimentation
* Changes made throughout development
* Technologies and tools used
* Lessons learned
* Future work or unfinished tasks

## Why I Keep Mind Maps

Projects don't always go exactly as planned. Some of the most valuable things I learn come from things that **didn't work**.

Instead of only documenting the finished product, these mind maps allow me to keep a history of the development process, including the mistakes and failures that helped me understand how to improve the project.

This repository is intended to show **how I worked through a problem**, not just the final solution.

## Repository Structure

Each project can have its own folder containing its corresponding mind map and supporting documentation.

```text
Project-Mind-Maps/
│
├── Project-Name/
│   ├── README.md
│   └── mind-map.md
│
├── Another-Project/
│   ├── README.md
│   └── mind-map.md
│
└── README.md
```

## What Each Mind Map Contains

The exact structure may vary depending on the project, but generally a mind map will contain:

### Project Overview

A brief explanation of the project, its purpose, and my role.

### Completed Work

A record of the features, tasks, or responsibilities that I completed.

### Successes

Things that worked well, successful implementations, and important accomplishments.

### Failures & Challenges

Problems I encountered, failed attempts, bugs, configuration issues, and other setbacks.

### Troubleshooting

The steps I took to investigate and resolve problems.

### Testing

Tests, experiments, simulated failures, and validation performed throughout the project.

### Lessons Learned

Important technical or practical lessons gained from the project.

### Future Work

Tasks that were planned but not completed, potential improvements, or areas that could be explored further.

## Projects

This repository will grow over time as I continue working on personal, academic, and professional projects.

The goal is to maintain a **living record of my development experience** and the process behind the projects I contribute to.
